# Asset and licence notes

## Main-project policy

- CC0: preferred
- CC-BY 4.0: acceptable
- CC-BY 3.0: disabled
- Custom / paid / unclear: rejected for the main project unless explicitly
  reconsidered

## Camera POV

No external assets. Only source changes in `config.ts`, `CameraFollow.ts` and
`PlayScene.ts`. Safe to promote.

## Vignette

The dryfit Vignette currently uses the LESED Analog Horror Screen FX sampler.
That pack is **custom-licensed**, not CC0/CC-BY.

LESED terms (summary):

- usable in personal and commercial projects, including games
- may modify/recolour/crop/retime/combine
- may not sell or share the overlay files themselves, alone or modified
- may not use the files to train or fine-tune generative AI models
- credit appreciated but not required

Under the current main-project policy this is **not approved for main** without
an explicit exception.

Main-safe options to decide later:

1. Generate an original pixel-stepped vignette asset that we own.
2. Use a CC0 or CC-BY 4.0 vignette.
3. Implement the Vignette as original CSS/code only.
4. Make a deliberate policy exception for LESED.

No decision has been made. The Vignette is pinned in `STATUS.md`.

## Scanlines / Static / REC HUD

Same LESED custom licence. Dryfit-only until the Vignette decision is made.
